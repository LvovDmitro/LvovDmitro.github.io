When the history
is wrong.

SCFR · Longitudinal radiology

Paper ↗ Code ↗ CPU demo ↗

Prior images and reports can help generate a new radiology report. They can also introduce the wrong information. SCFR selects between current-only and history-conditioned report proposals without a reference report at inference.

My contribution I originated the idea and led the research and manuscript.

I evaluated routing under deliberately mismatched histories, including the trade-off between reference-based false positives and false negatives.

02

NeurIPS 2025 · First author

Learning from a
radiologist's attention.

LogitGaze-Med · Human attention

Paper ↗ Code ↗

LogitGaze-Med combines chest X-ray features, diagnostic text, and semantic priors from a medical vision-language model to predict fixation locations and dwell times. We also evaluate predicted gaze as supervision for pathology classification.

My contribution I co-developed the medical VLM priors for scanpath prediction and the study of predicted gaze as additional supervision.

03

ICLR 2025 workshop · First author

Predicting visual search
from model semantics.

LogitGaze · Human visual search

Paper ↗ Code ↗

Our earlier work studies human visual search in natural images. LogitGaze extracts word-level semantic information from a vision-language model through the logit lens and uses it to predict human attention. This work provides the general-image counterpart to the medical-imaging direction developed in LogitGaze-Med.

Authorship First author, with Ilya Pershin.

ICLR 2025 Workshop on Reasoning and Planning for Large Language Models.

From predictions to patient selection

Similar scores can mean
different clinical workloads.

I design clinical evaluation studies with students and am second author on both papers.

For the EHR study, we compare how patient-history representations change high-risk episode selection while differences in aggregate predictive performance remain uncertain. I designed the study, supervised the student's initial experiments, then validated and extended the analysis and wrote the paper.

For the referral study, we separate how eligibility rules and capacity limits change workload at fixed risk scores. I originated the study and designed the experiments. The student ran the original experiments and prepared the first draft; I checked the results and revised the manuscript.
